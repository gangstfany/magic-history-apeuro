# Question-led causal and comparative paths

Expanded on 2026-10-29 from one example per Unit to three: 27 paths and 150 steps. Existing paths remain available. Four explicitly labeled comparison paths examine different outcomes without implying that one national case caused the next. The other paths identify causes, conditions, decisions and consequences. These are teacher-authored analytical summaries, not official exam questions or unique official interpretations.

## Source and interpretive checks

Each step references an existing card in the same Unit, retaining its CED, AMSCO and user-card locators. New connections were written from those card summaries/details; no 32-lesson materials were used. Group-level references do not certify every clause as a textbook quotation. The original source-screening limits in content-issues.md still apply.

Specific safeguards: German/Italian and early-modern state-building cases are comparisons, not serial causes; Catholic reforms do not imply doctrinal abolition of indulgences; the Scientific Revolution path notes overlapping methods and institutions rather than treating the numbered sequence as exact chronology; labor legislation and worker organization partly overlap; European integration continues across the oil-shock era; crisis and war are not sufficient explanations for Nazism or genocide. New text avoids introducing precise new historical counts or dates beyond the referenced cards.
